# Music Plan

Arc: Mysterious pulse → epic lift → inspirational resolve

- 0–3s: Low pulse and heartbeat, leave room for the hook.
- 3–15s: Add percussive research ticks and a restrained bass movement.
- 15–35s: Open the orchestral bed as the knowledge graph and product reveal arrive.
- Final beat: Brief silence before the CTA, then an inspirational resolve.

Sound effects: heartbeat, paper impact, data ticks, whoosh transitions, particle shimmer, interface clicks, low sub hit, logo air release.
