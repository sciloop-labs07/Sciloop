**Tagline:** Enter the control room of humanity.

**Description:** SciLoop turns live scientific discoveries into explorable stories. Follow the signal, understand the mechanism, see the people and technologies behind it, and imagine what could happen next.

We are launching with a focused live interface and want early users to tell us where the journey feels confusing or magical.

## Demo video script — 75 seconds

1. Open the homepage: “This is the live activity of humanity.”
2. Click **LIVE INNOVATIONS**.
3. Open one discovery card.
4. Click **Explain with SciLoop AI**.
5. Show the explanation overlay and visual action.
6. Show timeline, scientists, technology, and future possibility sections.
7. Return to the Innovation Universe.
8. Close with: “Don’t just read what humanity discovered. Explore what it could become.”

## User interview script

Ask every early user:

1. What did you think SciLoop was in the first ten seconds?
2. What did you click first, and why?
3. Where did you hesitate?
4. Which part made the innovation clearer?
5. Which part felt decorative or confusing?
6. What would make you return tomorrow?
7. Who would you send this to?

Record exact language, not just a score.
