# Enterprise Demo Script

## Audience

Corporate innovation, R&D, strategy, foresight, or learning leaders. Keep the demo to 20–30 minutes and use one customer-relevant domain.

## 1. Start with a live signal

“This is the signal the team is trying to understand. We preserve the source, date, field, and uncertainty before generating an explanation.”

Show the source-linked signal and the question it creates.

## 2. Open the evidence map

Show the knowledge graph: entities, mechanism, history, organizations, applications, open questions, and source-linked claims.

Say: “The graph makes the reasoning inspectable. It does not turn a missing source into a fact.”

## 3. Show causal variables

Identify the variables that can change, the dependencies that constrain them, and the assumptions the customer can challenge.

## 4. Compare scenarios

Show supported, plausible, and speculative scenarios. For each, show trigger, causal chain, required conditions, benefits, risks, unknowns, and falsifiers.

## 5. Run the visual model

Change one variable. Explain what moved, what caused the effect, and what the model is not claiming. Use the smallest useful renderer with a fallback.

## 6. Review risks and unknowns

Ask the customer which assumption they would test first. Record disagreement instead of hiding it.

## 7. End with a decision summary

Return:

- What the evidence supports.
- What remains conditional.
- What would change the conclusion.
- The next experiment or data request.
- The measurable pilot outcome.

## Close

“SciLoop is not asking you to trust a black-box forecast. It gives your team a shared, evidence-linked way to inspect what may happen under explicit conditions.”
