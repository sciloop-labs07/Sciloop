# Enterprise Value Metrics

## Measurement principles

Measure baseline before pilot launch. Keep structural engine scores separate from empirical user or outcome evidence. Report sample size, method, date range, and uncertainty with every metric.

## Core metrics

| Metric | Definition | Collection method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time to first usable explanation | Time from signal intake to reviewed explanation. | Baseline interview plus run timestamps. |
| Research synthesis time | Human hours required to create the equivalent brief. | Time study and activity log. |
| Evidence coverage | Share of material claims linked to approved sources. | Source/claim audit. |
| Scenario completion | Share of users who complete a scenario run. | Product event log. |
| Comprehension | User can explain the mechanism and key assumption. | Short rubric-based assessment. |
| Transfer | User can apply the model to a new case. | Scenario task or structured interview. |
| Decision confidence | Self-reported confidence with reason and uncertainty. | Pre/post survey; not proof of correctness. |
| Reuse | Number of later briefs, workshops, or decisions reusing the model. | Workspace records. |
| Prediction error | Difference between recorded expectation and later observed result. | Versioned prediction/outcome record. |

## Guardrails

- Do not call engagement a business outcome without customer agreement.
- Do not call a structural score accuracy.
- Do not compare pilots with different audiences or baselines without qualification.
- Do not promote a learning rule until the sample size and evidence threshold are defined.
- Preserve negative results and failed scenarios.
